Equity Peer Correlation

How closely each fund's daily returns move with its category peers over a trailing window. Values near 1 mean two funds rise and fall almost together.

SEBI Reg. No.: MF/084/25/10
Period (point to point)

Correlation of daily returns

Each fund against its peers

Same category and period as the matrix. Average is the mean correlation with every other fund in the matrix.

Method. Pearson correlation of daily NAV returns (NAV on a day ÷ NAV on the previous market day − 1) for every pair of funds, over the trailing window ending on the as-of date. A fund is included only if it has NAV history for the whole window, so recent launches appear in the shorter periods only. NAVs are not carried forward over missing days; a pair needs at least 70% of the window's days in common. Within one category most pairs sit between 0.85 and 0.99, so the colour scale starts at 0.60 and anything lower takes the lightest shade.

Scope. Open-ended equity categories under para 3.8 of the SEBI Master Circular for Mutual Funds dated 20 March 2026. Sectoral and Thematic funds are left out because funds in those categories hold different sectors and are not like-for-like peers. Category is each scheme's current AMFI classification.

Source: AMFI India (daily NAV, direct growth plans).

Correlation describes how returns moved together in the past period shown. It says nothing about the level of returns and is not an indication of future co-movement.
